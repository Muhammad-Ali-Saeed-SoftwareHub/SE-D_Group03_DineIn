Catering FAQs

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend at least 5 business days for Basic/Premium packages and 2 weeks for Deluxe grand banquets.

Is delivery and setup included?

Yes, standard delivery and chafing setup within a 15-mile radius are included in all packages.

Can dishes be swapped or customized?

Absolutely. Our chef will gladly substitute proteins, sides, or spice levels to fit your vision.

What is your cancellation policy?

Catering inquiries may be rescheduled or canceled up to 72 hours before your date without penalty.
